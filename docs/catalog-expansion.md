## Source method

Exact public product pages, Shopify product/variant feeds and WooCommerce Store API records supply prices, selected variants, package descriptions, availability snapshots and photographs. Search snippets were discovery only. The source lines retained here come from nine retailer names after treating “EvoBikes” and “Evobikes” as one store: Bike Addict, EvoBikes, Olympic Cycles, Finish Line Cycles, Complete Cyclist, Biket Hillcrest, RA Cycles South Africa, Aero Agencies South Africa and Bruce Reyneke Cycles. Other stores and manufacturer sources were researched or used for specification context; see the coverage object for each batch. There is no claim to have covered all South African stores.

Bike Addict currency was also checked against its public ZAR cart endpoint. Reference prices are full product prices, not finance instalments. Each numeric product price is the sum of its charged source-unit prices multiplied by quantities. A tyre individually sold is represented as quantity two for the build. Bar/stem bundles retain separate priced source lines. Reference-only package lines show the parent offer for evidence and are not charged again.

Local source photographs retain their attribution and original remote URL. Attribution records provenance; it does not assert a reuse licence. A complete-bike photograph may illustrate a frame-only offer, and the package caption and price basis govern what is included. Source availability, including explicit sold-out offers, is a dated snapshot and must be reconfirmed before sourcing.

## Package pricing and scope

All 11 new drivetrain choices are unsplittable retailer groupset references. Partial electronic upgrade kits were excluded rather than represented as full drivetrains. Controls are described in the chosen packages, so their price must not be added independently. The brake step distinguishes confirmation from a verified inclusion:

- **Force D2, Force E1 and RED AXS:** the exact composition lists identify left/right controls and front/rear calipers. Their matching brake checks carry a raw catalog price of `null`; the configured build resolves the check to **R0 included** only with its exact parent groupset. R0 is a contextual no-additional-charge result, not a free standalone brake set.
- **Ultegra, Dura-Ace and six Campagnolo packages:** caliper quantities or inclusion are not established by the available bill of materials. Their matching checks remain unpriced pending supplier confirmation. Ultegra’s recommended BR-R8170 caliper is not proof that it is supplied.
- **Bianchi Oltre XR4 Disc and LOOK 795 Blade RS:** two frame-bound cockpit checks similarly resolve to included only with the exact frame package. A separately selected frame does not inherit an included cockpit. The LOOK frame itself has no verified price and remains quote-required.

An independently priced replacement chain can remove the original mechanical 105 bundle’s explicit R490 chain line. The three confirmed SRAM packages include chains in an unsplittable price: a separately purchased replacement adds cost while the original packaged chain remains included; no made-up credit is deducted. A spare always adds a separately purchased chain. Unresolved package contents remain a supplier check.

The RED offer is marked sold out and includes a 172.5 mm 50/37 power-meter crank, 10–33 cassette, chain, both brake assemblies, Paceline X 160/160 rotors, two batteries, charger and Hammerhead Karoo. Selecting duplicate rotors or electronics as extras still adds those purchases; included accessories are not silently substituted or credited. Force E1 explicitly excludes BB and rotors. Its generic power-meter prose does not establish a power meter in the actual supplied composition.

## Families and compatibility

Control-system labels distinguish Shimano mechanical 12-speed, Shimano Di2 12-speed, SRAM AXS 12-speed, Campagnolo mechanical 12-speed, Campagnolo EPS 12-speed and Campagnolo wireless 13-speed. Matching speed or brand alone does not establish controls or chain interchangeability. Known conflicting control-system families and known required-freehub mismatches are reported as conflicts. A nominal family agreement remains a supplier review for the exact parts.

New drivetrain chain families are `shimano-hg12`, `sram-flattop12`, `campagnolo-12` and `campagnolo-13`. Separately listed chains also cover documented legacy 10/11-speed standards for spares or future sourcing; installing them in a current 12/13-speed drivetrain produces a speed/family conflict where documented. KMC cross-brand compatibility is restricted to each exact listing’s standard claims. Eagle/T-Type is not Road Flattop. Four insufficiently supported or MTB-only chain offers remain collection-only; no new standalone Campagnolo 13-speed chain offer was verified in this pass.

Source-backed required freehubs are recorded for the three SRAM packages (XDR), Dura-Ace (Shimano road 11/12-speed interface) and Super Record 13 (N3W). Other unverified interface fields stay unknown rather than inferred from a logo. The UI checks known frame/brake, clearance, axle, tyre system and bar/stem clamp facts where both sides are documented. Every build still requires review of size, exact generation, BB shell/spindle, routing, mounts, rail clamps, rotor/hub interface and installation supplies. Selecting a BB, rotor or generic accessory does not automatically certify it for the build.

## Variants, measurements and ratings

The expansion adds 53 option registers and 47 feature registers. A field can offer only the source-backed values registered for that product. An exact reviewed SKU combination supplies its own dated price and URL; a requested or unverified combination stays quote-required. A list of catalogue crank lengths, colours or gear ratios is not automatically a priced set of orderable combinations. Size labels are source labels, not a fit recommendation.

Variant changes clear reference-only mass and other unsupported claims unless exact variant facts restore them. A bare-frame claim, a handlebar-only measurement, a catalogue example at another width/size or a chain measured at a different link count is not a delivered package mass. All 11 new groupset package masses remain unknown. Thirty-six expansion references retain a numeric source-scoped mass; this is not a promise that all such masses belong in the partial build total. That total continues to count only its explicitly supported selected package categories and supported fitted extras.

No new stiffness, comfort, CdA, drag-watt or complete-bike speed values are invented. Existing source-backed purpose/depth/mass profiles and editorial goal-match rules apply within their documented limits. Aero marketing and carbon grade are not wind-tunnel measurements. The research lab’s wheel-mass gravity model remains the only supported power-delta estimate.
