## Review method and limits

Specialized South Africa regional category records supplied model names, model year, product-specific URLs, images and explicit ZAR MSRP. Manufacturer MSRP remains MSRP even when a swatch advertises a promotional price. Bike Addict public Shopify product responses supplied product descriptions, images and exact selected variant IDs, prices in integer cents, and availability snapshots. Search suggestions were used only for discovery. Manufacturer-photo references are credited to Specialized; retailer-photo references are credited to Bike Addict rather than asserting manufacturer ownership of every retailer image.

The JSON retains each original image URL, credited product page, source name, quantity, date and reference variant. Added photos are unmodified source copies. Variant-linked photos are preferred when supplied. Confirm supplier authorization for commercial image use; attribution alone does not establish a licence or brand partnership.

Reference sizes are not automatic fit recommendations. MSRP and retailer prices are distinguished. Online availability does not establish RCS stock or confirmed supply. Prices do not auto-refresh. Confirm VAT treatment, final specification, size/colour, axle/freehub, tire/rim and pressure compatibility, assembly/delivery and package contents. Some listings have unspecified freehub options: no universal compatibility is claimed. Campagnolo N3W wheel listings were excluded from this Shimano builder expansion.

Tires are consistently priced as pairs. Cockpits include a bar plus a separately sourced 100 mm PLT stem; their photo shows the bar. Drivetrain packages differ in scope; do not assume equivalent inclusions from price alone. The Di2 controls/calipers package check remains unpriced to avoid double-counting controls already described by the full groupset listing. Complete-bike prices never enter a custom component subtotal.
