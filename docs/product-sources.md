## Review method and limits

Specialized regional product/category pages exposed model names, MSRP in ZAR and the corresponding product images; individual frameset pages were also reviewed for included parts, product weights and explicitly stated tire clearance. Bike Addict public product pages and their Shopify product JSON supplied selected variant IDs, prices (integer cents in the `.js` responses), availability flags and listing photos. Search suggestions were used for discovery only, not as the final price record.

The JSON data retains each original image URL, credited product page, source name, quantity and price. Local photos are unmodified copies; source attribution does not assert a brand partnership or blanket image licence. Confirm supplier image authorization for commercial use.

Manufacturer MSRP is intentionally not represented as a current discounted dealer offer. Online listing availability is not RCS stock. Full compatibility, exact frame/colour/size, VAT treatment, assembly/delivery and final inclusions require quotation. The Di2 brake package remains unpriced because separately confirmed caliper inclusions are absent. No fabricated performance ratings or complete-bike weight estimates are supplied.
