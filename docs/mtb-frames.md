# MTB frames and frame kits

Reviewed 19 October 2026. There are 49 frame references across 13 brands, up from 15. The 25 reviewed South African prices are reference package prices, not RCS inventory. The other 24 models use manufacturer technical evidence and require an SA supplier quote. No foreign prices are converted. The builder sorts priced references first and supports separate brand, material, purpose, suspension and frame-source filters.

Exact source sizes/colours/packages are selectable only where verified. Manufacturer model ranges are not local SKU inventory; request sizing privately for a supplier quote. Photos may show complete bikes, another colour or included/excluded shocks: the card and inspection labels identify the actual quoted scope. No delivered frame mass is invented from an unspecified bare-frame claim.

Read [package accounting, compatibility and validation](mtb-builder.md). PYGA bare/kit variants preserve shock inclusion; Stealth Medium does not borrow contradictory Large facts; Banshee V3.2 generations stay separate; Cotic BFe UDH does not permit SRAM Transmission. Size-specific wheel setups, shock tunes/hardware, approved forks and availability require exact supplier checks.

## Research boundaries

This expansion adds ten publicly priced SA references and 24 manufacturer references. Sources include PYGA, Evo Bikes, Cycles Direct and explicitly brand-new business/dealer Bike Hub offers, plus Yeti, Ibis, Transition, Banshee and Cotic technical pages. Complete PYGA builds, used/private offers, motor-assisted frames and unverifiable/blocked model pages are excluded. The coverage is substantial rather than exhaustive. Manufacturer quote-only choices do not establish SA distribution, local stock, final landed price or exact kit contents. Quote/download exports retain missing essentials and source URLs.
