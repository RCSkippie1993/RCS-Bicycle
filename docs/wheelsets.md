## Selection and evidence

Public road-wheel categories/search pages were inspected at each included retailer. Exact product/variant pages supplied titles, prices, package contents, available photos and specifications. Shopify variant prices are actual selected variants, not a collection minimum; WooCommerce sale prices replace crossed-out prices. CWC/Cycle Lab prices come from the product page. Store currency was checked as ZAR. No membership payment plan or instalment amount is used as the product price.

References retain original source URLs, exact variant descriptions, observation dates, product photo URLs and local downloaded images. Front/rear Roval wheels sold separately use two source lines and a summed pair price; the photo may show one wheel and is labelled accordingly. A single tire or wheel is never silently doubled into an assumed package. Unavailable listings remain unavailable in the snapshot, and demo condition is explicit. No RCS availability, wholesale price or resale margin is asserted.

Fields are recorded only when the exact listing has reviewed wording. Material groups distinguish carbon, alloy and carbon/alloy laminate. A model number is not enough to assign depth. Mixed-depth front/rear pairs have no single-depth score. A clincher listing is not automatically approved for tubeless. Freehub alternatives without a selected or title-specific standard stay unconfirmed. Marketing performance statements are not converted into speed, stiffness or aero measurements.

Only reviewed Shimano disc-road variants join the road builder. Every listed interface still requires exact package/fit review. Rim-brake and other freehub standards, quick-release hubs, gravel applications and conflicting variants remain collection references. Single rear TT/disc wheels were held back rather than misrepresented as pairs. Expanding into TT/MTB wheelsets remains future work.

Buycycle returned an empty category; Bike Mob search did not expose verifiable offers; Cycle Factory returned a landing page. These results establish no claim about their actual store inventory. Additional retailers and future listing updates require another dated review.
