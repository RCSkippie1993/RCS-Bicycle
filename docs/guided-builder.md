## Variant scope and arithmetic

Exact recorded Shopify SKUs have source price, option values, variant URL and observed orderability. Orderability is never a stock commitment. Factor/ENVE/S5 options list colours without documented frame sizes; size requests need supplier confirmation. Cervélo Soloist source labels “54mm” / “58mm” are reproduced, not converted into verified geometry or fitting advice. Aethos original-generation size variants explicitly preserve unavailable flags.

Winspace arrays identify source attributes, not a verified cartesian product of orderable combinations. Only the recorded reference combination has a price in this register; another size/colour combination is unpriced until verified. Size M Agile enforces the sourced 28 mm nominal limit. Larger/other-size assumptions do not silently establish clearance.

Bar-width and PLT stem-length choices retain separate source variants and prices; stems span source-listed 60–120 mm. Clamp/steerer/routing/fit need review. Crank/gearing choices retain the other recorded bundle lines; changing a crank variant does not silently change cassette, derailleurs or chain. Source photos may depict another variant.

The itemised mechanical bundle includes a R490 M7100 116-link chain. Replacing it removes that one source line and adds the selected chain; a spare keeps it. Di2 has no itemised chain credit, so a separate installed replacement adds its reference price while a package/credit check remains unpriced. SRAM RED Road Flattop cannot become an approved installed chain in these Shimano bundles. A spare is not installed. KMC's broad retailer claim remains a supplier-review item, not blanket approval for every SRAM/Shimano system.

## Live documented mass

Only reviewed selected frame / wheel / tyre package masses and supported accessory masses are summed. Bare-frame claims, partial bar-only facts, unverified stem/chain-length masses, unknown component masses and spare chains are excluded. The result is a **partial documented package-mass subtotal**, never a complete-bike weight. Known rows and their measurement bases remain inspectable. Components already in packages require an inclusion review; the feature does not promise a complete bill of materials.

## Research lab

The wheel-mass gravity delta is:

`watts saved = (reference pair kg − selected pair kg) × 9.80665 × (km/h ÷ 3.6) × sin(arctan(gradient percent ÷ 100))`.

Positive means less gravitational power at that constant speed/gradient. Zero gradient or equal mass gives zero; a heavier selection gives a power increase. Inputs are limited to 5–60 km/h and 0–20% gradient. Both pair masses require reviewed excerpts and source URLs. Example: 1.810 kg R470 versus 1.420 kg RD38 at 15 km/h / 6% gives approximately **0.95 W** less gravity-related wheel power. It is not an aero saving or complete riding-power prediction.

[Martin, Milliken, Cobb, McFadden and Coggan (1998), *Validation of a Mathematical Model for Road Cycling Power*, Journal of Applied Biomechanics 14(3), 276–291](https://doi.org/10.1123/jab.14.3.276) supports the resistive-power modelling framework. Its abstract and bibliographic record were reviewed through Crossref on 15 October 2026. This implementation isolates the basic gravitational term; it does not replicate the entire validated model or inherit the paper's reported error bounds. Rolling loss, drivetrain efficiency, acceleration, changes in rider position and aero forces are excluded. Listed weights can use different conditions.

[NASA Glenn, *Drag Equation*](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/drag-equation/) explains density, velocity, coefficient and reference area. A rim-depth number or product purpose does not provide matched CdA data for the selected bicycle and rider. No matched aero product comparison is recorded here, so aerodynamic watt savings remain pending. No marketing watt number is transferred across products, yaw angles, tyre widths or test speeds.
